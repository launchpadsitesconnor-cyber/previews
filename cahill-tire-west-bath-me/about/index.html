About us

Same corner of the trade since 1938

We opened in Bath in 1938. The owners have changed, the shop has grown, and we're still a place where you pull in with a tire problem and get a straight answer.

EST. 1938West Bath, ME
  1. 1938

    We open in Bath

    The shop opens its doors to local drivers, and it's been part of the Bath area ever since.

  2. 1958

    Tire selling begins

    A second generation joins the business and starts selling tires. That's the start of the trade we're known for.

  3. 2012

    A handoff to the next generation

    The business stays in the family as it passes to the founder's grandchildren.

  4. April 2025

    New ownership

    The shop changes hands again. The name, the trade and the promise on new tires stay the same.

  5. Now

    A full-service garage

    Tires are still the heart of it. We also do brakes, batteries and oil changes. The only things we don't do are state inspections and alignments.

Today

A small shop that does a lot

We sell and mount tires for cars, trucks, ATVs and trailers, and we stock heavy-duty wheels and tires up to 30". We repair flats, rotate and balance, service tire pressure sensors, fill with nitrogen and mount low profile tires.

Past tires, we handle brakes, batteries and oil changes. And if you buy your tires here, air and rotations are free for as long as you own them.

Around here

Part of the neighborhood

We put money back into the area through our local chamber and recreational programs. The people we fix cars for live and work nearby, so it only makes sense.

Drivers tell us they like the friendly, fast service, the fair prices and the neighborly feel, and that we're there when they're stuck. We're glad to hear it, and we try to earn it every day.

What we don't do

Alignments and state inspections

We'd rather say so up front than have you make the trip for nothing. For everything else on the car, call us.
