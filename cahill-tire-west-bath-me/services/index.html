Not sure what you need?

Start with what you notice

You don't need the right word for it. Tell us what the car is doing and we'll take a look.

Losing air or a nail in the tread

That's a flat repair. We'll patch it if it's safe to, and replace the tire if it's not.

Squealing, grinding or a soft pedal

That's brakes. We'll check them and replace worn pads, rotors and parts.

Slow starts or a dead battery

We'll test the battery and swap in a new one if it needs it.

Tire pressure light stuck on? That one's TPMS. We'll service and reset the sensors.

What we don't do

Skip the drive for these two

We don't do alignments or state inspections. We do everything else on the car, and we do it before 5.

  • Alignments
