Questions

Quick answers

What tire brands do you carry?

General Tire, Pirelli, Michelin, Camso, Sailun, Continental, Yokohama, Falken, Firestone and Bridgestone. Call and we'll tell you what fits your vehicle and budget.

Do you do state inspections or alignments?

Not at this time. We handle tires and full-service auto repair except for state inspections and alignments.

Do I get anything with new tires?

Yes. Free air and free tire rotations for the life of any tires you buy from us.

How do I order tires?

Call us or stop in. We'll find the right tire for your vehicle and budget and set up a time to install it.
