Stopping in

Look for the gas station

We're right beside it and we share its lot. Park there and walk in.

Picked up a nail or hearing a squeal? You don't need to call first. Pull in and tell us what's going on. If you'd rather know we have your tire size before you drive over, a quick call does that.

We're open weekdays only, so Saturday and Sunday trips won't find anyone here.

Before you call

Have these ready

Two minutes of looking saves a lot of back and forth on the phone.

  • Your tire size
    It's molded into the sidewall, something like 205/55R16. Not sure how to read it? The sidewall decoder shows what each number means.
  • Or the year, make and model
    If you can't get to the tire, your vehicle's year, make and model works just as well for tires.
  • What it's doing
    Losing air, squealing brakes, slow starts. A plain description is plenty.
  • What you want to spend
    For new tires, tell us how you drive and your budget, and we'll match a brand to both.
  • Whether you bought the tires here
    Tires bought from us get free air and free rotations for as long as you own them.
