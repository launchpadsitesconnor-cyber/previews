Baked for your order

We’ve been serving Los Angeles since 1996, and our menu has taken a new direction since 2011. That mix shows up in the food: Ponchik, Perashki, Armenian Pizza, Georgian Khachapuri, Jingalov Bread, and cakes that bring classic pastries a modern spin. Our menu reflects a wide mix of tastes, so there’s something here for breakfast, lunch, dessert, or a quick snack. If you’re after the popular Nutella ponchik or something savory like Cheese Borek or Adjarakan, we make it fresh when you order.
