About

Beer first. Burgers a close second.

Mash Tun is a casual craft beer pub in Portland's Old Port, with burgers, pub food, cocktails and plenty on tap.

The place

Easy room, loud enough.

Food comes out in red-checked paper boats. The bar is butcher block.

We're in the Old Port and we've built the place around the neighborhood: quick stops, game nights and longer hangs with friends. Nobody's going to rush you either way.

The menu leans burger bar. Burgers, seafood, sandwiches and snacks, with beer on tap and cocktails next to them. Order a full meal, or a few small plates and another round.

Start with fried pickles or oysters. Move on to the Mash Tun Burger, the Maine Burger or a lobster roll. Add fries. Pick a taco if you're still going. It's all on the menu page, prices included.
