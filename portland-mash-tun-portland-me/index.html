About

Casual, easy, stay a while.

We're in Portland's Old Port, running a laid-back bar with burgers, pub food, cocktails and a rotating mix of beer on tap. Come in for a quick bite, a late-night meal, or a few drinks with friends while the game's on.

The menu runs from the Mash Tun Burger and the Maine Burger to lobster rolls, wings, fried pickles, oysters and grilled cheese. There's a breakfast sandwich, a Caesar, fries and tacos too. Order a full meal or a few small plates.

  • Casual atmosphereLaid-back, so it works for a quick bite or a longer stay.

Quick answers

What kind of food do you serve?

Burgers, pub food, sandwiches, seafood, salads and snacks like wings, fries, fried pickles and oysters.

Do you serve beer and cocktails?

Yes. There's beer on tap, and we make cocktails too.

Do you serve late-night food?

Yes. Late-night food is one of our things, and the hours run late on weekends.
