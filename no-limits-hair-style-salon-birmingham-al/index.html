Our salon

No Limits Hair & Style Salon has been part of Birmingham since 1981. Cindy Ferraro owns the salon, and it’s still individually owned and operated, with Peter Ferraro as part of the story. We opened because we wanted a place where hair care feels more like art than a chain routine. People come to us for personalized cuts, custom color, and a friendly, accepting atmosphere that works for all hair types.
