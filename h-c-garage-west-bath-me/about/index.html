About the shop

A Subaru shop on State Road

Subaru work is where we really shine. We take care of other cars and trucks too, from figuring out what that new noise is to brake jobs and routine maintenance.

The focus

Why Subarus

A Subaru tends to stay in the driveway a long time, and the same jobs come up as the miles add up: head gaskets, wheel bearings, CV axles, timing work. That's the work we know best.

Knowing the car means less guessing. When you tell us it hums on the highway or clunks on a tight turn, we usually have a good idea where to look.

And if you don't drive a Subaru, bring it anyway. We work on most cars and trucks.

How we work

Three things we don't skip

  1. 01

    We explain it first

    We'll tell you what's wrong, what it costs and what can wait. Then you decide.

  2. 02

    We call before extra work

    If we find something you didn't bring it in for, you hear about it from us before anything gets done. We keep you posted the whole way.

  3. 03

    We charge a fair price

    The work gets done quickly and priced fairly. That's what people mention most about us.
