House details

We’re in a historic building in Boulder that was built before the turn of the century, with a setting shaped by Zen practice and stillness. Inside, you’ll find original artwork, local artisan custom-designed beds, radiant floor heating, skylights, bathtubs, and balcony seating in some rooms. Guests can use the Zen meditation room, join scheduled meditation periods and other programs, and spend time in the shared gardens. It’s a good fit when you want a quieter stay with thoughtful details and room to breathe.
