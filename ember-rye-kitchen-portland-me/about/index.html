Our story

We built the room around the fire.

Ember & Rye Kitchen is a wood-fired kitchen in Portland, ME. This is why we cook the way we do, and what we hope your evening feels like.

Portland, ME

Wood-fired meals, Portland nights.

We cook over a wood fire because nothing else gives us what we want on the plate. The heat has moods. It flares, it settles, it gets a little stubborn. We'd rather work with that than flatten it.

Our menu leans on that fire from start to finish. You'll taste the warmth in dishes that feel comforting and still come across bright. Char on the edges. A little smoke underneath. We think that's plenty to be proud of.

Ember, and Rye

Ember is the easy half of the name. It's the glow you see when the flames settle, and it's where the cooking happens.

Rye is the other half. We think of it as the color of the room: wheat gold, like candlelight on a wooden table. Put them together and you get the whole idea. Something hot to cook on, something warm to sit in.

Portland nights

We're at home in Portland, ME, and we like meals that match the mood of the city. Cooking for the coast means food that's warm and satisfying, with something bright next to it so it never feels heavy.

The weather matters to us. When it turns cool and the candles come out, the fire stops being a kitchen tool and becomes the best seat in the house. That's the evening we cook for.

The fire doesn't take requests. That's why it tastes like this.

At the table

Then you sit down.

We cook for a night that starts with a seat at the table and ends with one more round of conversation. Nobody's counting how long you've been here.

Come hungry. Bring someone good to talk to. Settle in and see what's on the fire.
