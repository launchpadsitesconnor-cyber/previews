Good to know

A few answers.

What does wood-fired mean?

We cook over burning wood and coals, not a gas flame or an electric element. The heat is livelier and less even, which is where the char and the smoke come from.

Why is the menu sorted by char, roast, smoke and coals?

Because that's how the fire works. Some food goes straight over the flames, some roasts slowly in the heat around them, some picks up smoke, and some finishes on the coals. It's easier to choose that way than by course.

Does the menu change with the season?

Yes. What's on the fire shifts as the weather does, and the food leans warmer and more comforting once Portland nights turn cool.

Will I smell like woodsmoke afterward?

Possibly a little. Most people count that as part of the evening.
