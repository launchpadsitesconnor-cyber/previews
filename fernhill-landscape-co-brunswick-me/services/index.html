Services · Brunswick, ME

Five jobs, and the season each one belongs to

A yard asks for different things at different times. Here's what we do, what you get, and when it makes the most sense.

Spring · Apr–Jun

01

Planting

New shrubs and perennials go in at the right depth, in a spot that suits the light and the soil. We dig the hole, set the plant, firm the soil around it and water it in, so it starts out settled rather than perched.

You getA bed that looks planted on purpose, not dropped in on a weekend.

Best time Apr–Jun, Sep–Nov
02

Mulching

Mulch goes down once, and goes down right. We spread an even layer across the bed, pull it back from trunks and stems, and tidy the edge where it meets the lawn.

You getBeds that look finished from the street. Your beds will thank you by July.

Best time Apr–Jun

Summer · Jun–Aug

03

Lawn mowing

We cut the lawn the way you'd cut it if you had the Saturday. Even height, clean lines across the grass, and the clippings and stray bits swept off walks and drives when we're done.

You getA lawn that looks cared for, without giving up your weekend to it.

Best time Jun–Aug
04

Trimming and edging

Hedges and shrubs get shaped back to their natural form, not clipped into boxes. Then we cut a sharp line where lawn meets bed, and along walks and drives, where grass likes to creep.

You getCrisp edges that make the whole yard look pulled together.

Best time Jun–Aug

Fall · Sep–Nov

05

Yard cleanup

Leaves, fallen branches and the general debris of a season, cleared off the lawn and out of the beds. It's the job for a yard that needs a fresh start, and it's just as useful in spring, once the snow has gone and shown what winter left behind.

You getA clear lawn and open beds, ready for winter or ready for the first mow.

Best time Sep–Nov, and Apr
