Good to know

Quick answers

The short version of what we do and where.

Where do you work?

We do landscape work for yards in Brunswick, ME.

What kind of work do you do?

Lawn mowing, trimming and edging, planting, mulching and yard cleanup. Each one has its own write-up on the services page.

I'm not sure what my yard needs. Can I still get in touch?

Yes. Describe what you see in the notes box, or tick whatever sounds closest. Sorting out the right jobs for the yard is part of the visit.

When is the right time for each job?

Cleanup, planting and mulching lean toward spring. Mowing, trimming and edging run through summer. Fall brings another round of cleanup. The services page has a “best time” tag on every job.

What should I put in the form?

Your name, the street or neighborhood, and the jobs you have in mind. A line or two about the yard helps, but it's optional.
