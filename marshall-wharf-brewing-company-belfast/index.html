About the Brewery

Marshall Wharf Brewing Company is part of Belfast’s harbor-side character, offering a local spot for beer lovers in town. The focus here is on good beer and a relaxed place to enjoy it. If you’re visiting Belfast or already call it home, it’s a natural stop for a pint.
