About

Named for the rail yard.

Rigby Yard takes its name from the old Rigby rail yard, and the room still has that feel.

Brick, beams and a bar built for staying.

We're in a brick-walled former rail yard building in Portland's Old Port. Inside there's exposed brick, post and beam, big group tables and a bar built for staying a while.

Dinner is seasonal and rooted in Maine and New England, from braised short rib and steak frites to a proper lobster roll. The bar pours Maine craft beer, wine and cocktails, and weekdays start with espresso.

Then there's the soup. It's 33 cents a cup, one per guest.
