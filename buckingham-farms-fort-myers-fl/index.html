Farm and table

Buckingham Farms brings together a farm-to-table restaurant, a local market, and a rustic barn venue in Fort Myers. The land started as a hog farm in the 1950s, later became part of a citrus grove, and was bought by a local family in 2009. By 2012, it had grown into a full-service dining and event spot, with the barn opening in 2013. You can also order online, and we serve Fort Myers and Southwest Florida.
