Our bakery in Los Angeles

Sweet E's Bake Shop grew out of Erica’s kitchen, where baking started with her grandmother Mimi and her mom Momo. What began with family and friends turned into local dessert catering across Los Angeles, then a bakery at 4574 West Adams Boulevard in Los Angeles. We make custom cakes, logo and photo cookies, cupcakes, cake pops, cakesicles, and stuffed chocolate chip cookies. Many customers come to us for presentation that looks polished, packaging that holds up well in the mail, and desserts that are ready for birthdays, branding, and gifting.
