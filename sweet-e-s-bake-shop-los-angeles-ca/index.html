Our bakery story

Sweet E's Bake Shop started in Erica’s own kitchen, baking for family and friends before growing into local dessert catering around Los Angeles. Erica says her baking roots go back to her grandmother Mimi and her mom Momo, and that family feel still shows up in the cakes, cookies, and cupcakes we make today. You’ll find us on Robertson Boulevard near Beverly Hills, making custom-made desserts for events big and small. We also ship some items nationwide, so you can send a sweet gift even if you’re not nearby.
