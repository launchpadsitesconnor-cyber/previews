What the house is telling you

Four signs it's time to call.

Tap the one that sounds like yours.

SYMPTOM 01

Lights flicker

Dimming, pulsing or blinking, sometimes when something else switches on. Don't ignore it. It's usually the first thing a house tells you.

Troubleshooting → SYMPTOM 02

Breaker keeps tripping

A breaker that trips once is doing its job. One that trips again and again is pointing at a problem on that circuit, or at a panel that can't keep up.

Panel upgrades → SYMPTOM 03

Outlet or switch is dead

You plug in or flip it and nothing happens. Sometimes it's the device. Sometimes it's what's behind the plate.

Outlet and switch work → SYMPTOM 04

Switch plate feels warm

Warm to the touch isn't normal. Stop using that switch and have it looked at.
