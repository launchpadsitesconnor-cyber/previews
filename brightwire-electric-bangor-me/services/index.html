Panel schedule / Brightwire ElectricMain: ON

Fixing wiring, outlets, fixtures and connections that have stopped working the way they should. We find what failed, repair it and check the circuit around it before we leave.

When you need it
A circuit is dead, something buzzes or crackles, a plate or cover feels warm, or there's a scorch mark or burnt smell. Don't wait on any of those.
What we look at
The failed part, the connections on either side of it, and the condition of the wire at that spot.

New wiring for a space that doesn't have enough power, and rewiring for circuits that have outlived their wire. This is the work that ends up behind drywall, so it gets the most care.

When you need it
You're finishing a basement, adding a room or setting up a shop. Or the house has older wiring, or circuits that were added over the years without much of a plan.
What we look at
How the circuits are split up, the condition of existing wire and boxes, and every connection before the wall is closed.

Putting in new fixtures, swapping out old ones, and adding light to rooms that don't have enough of it. The aim is light that sits where you actually use the room, switched where your hand goes.

When you need it
A fixture is dated or failing, a room is too dark, or a remodel changes where the light should be.
What we look at
Where the power comes from, whether the box can carry the fixture, where the switch should go, and whether the bulbs and dimmer suit each other.

Replacing worn or dead outlets and switches, and adding new ones where you keep reaching for an extension cord. Small jobs, but they're the part of the system you touch every day.

When you need it
A plug won't hold, a switch is loose or does nothing, or you need power on a wall that has none.
What we look at
Loose or scorched connections, whether it's the device or the wire behind it that has failed, and what else is on the same circuit.

The panel is the box every circuit starts from. An upgrade swaps it for one that fits how you use power now, with breakers that are sized and labeled for what they run.

When you need it
Breakers trip over and over, there's no room for another circuit, or you're adding something that draws a lot of power.
What we look at
What the panel is carrying compared to what it can carry, the state of the connections inside, and whether the labels match the circuits.

Tracing a problem back to its cause when it isn't obvious. Flickering lights, a breaker that won't stay on, an outlet that works some days. We start from the symptom and work toward the fault.

When you need it
Something's wrong and you can't point to what. Or it's been fixed before and came back.
What we look at
The affected circuit from one end to the other: the device, the box, the run of wire and the breaker.
