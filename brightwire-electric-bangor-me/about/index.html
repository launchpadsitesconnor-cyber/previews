Our story

The part nobody sees.

Most of what we do ends up behind drywall, under a cover plate or inside a panel. Once it's closed up, nobody looks at it again.

That's why we pay attention to the details that matter after the walls are back together. We check what's behind the wall before we close it up, because you won't get another look.

We're a Bangor electrician. We take on repairs, wiring and rewiring, lighting, outlet and switch work, panel upgrades and troubleshooting, for homes and for businesses.

Lights flickering? Breaker tripping? Something just not working the way it should? Don't ignore it. It's usually the first thing a house tells you, and we're ready to take a look.

How we work

Three things we hold to.

  1. 1

    Solid work

    We build it to hold up after it's hidden. That means looking at what's already there before we add to it, and taking the time to do the part you'll never see.
