Before you write

Common questions.

What should I tell you?

Where the problem is, what it does, and when it started. "The hallway light flickers when the fridge kicks on" tells us more than "electrical problem." If you've noticed a smell, a buzzing sound or a warm plate, mention that too.

What does a visit look like?

We start where you first noticed the problem and work back from there: the switch, outlet or fixture, then the wiring and the panel behind it. We look at what's actually there before deciding what to do about it.

Why do my lights flicker?

It can be a loose connection, a bulb or fixture on its way out, or a circuit that's carrying more than it should. If it's one bulb, try swapping it. If several lights flicker, or it happens when an appliance starts, have it looked at.

My breaker keeps tripping. Is that bad?

A breaker that trips once is doing its job. One that trips again and again is telling you something about that circuit. Don't hold the breaker on or keep resetting it. Let us find out why it's tripping.

Do you work on businesses too?

Yes. We work on shops and offices as well as homes in Bangor. Tell us what kind of space it is when you write.
