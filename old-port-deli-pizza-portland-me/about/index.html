About us

Easy meals for downtown Portland.

Stacked deli sandwiches, hot pizza, and quick bites you can grab on the go.

Simple, filling, and made fresh.

We serve food people in Portland come back for when they want something simple, filling, and made fresh.

Our menu brings together deli counter comfort and pizza shop staples. Build a lunch that fits your day, or pick up dinner without slowing down.

Stop in when you want lunch, dinner, or something warm after a walk through the Old Port. We're right here, close to the pace of the neighborhood and ready when you are.

What's on the counter

Four things we do.

  1. 01

    Deli sandwiches

    Deli counter comfort that works for lunch or dinner. Filling and easy to carry, whether you want a classic sandwich or a faster meal between stops.

  2. 02

    Pizza

    A whole pie or by the slice, depending on what fits your day. Warm, filling, and ready to eat.

  3. 03

    Quick lunch plates

    Hot or cold plates for days when you want a meal that's fast but still feels complete, without a long sit-down.

  4. 04

    Grab-and-go bites

    Set up for pick up and keep moving. A snack, a lunch add-on, or a small meal on the run.
