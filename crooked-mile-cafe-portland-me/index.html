Good to know

Questions we get.

When is breakfast served?

Breakfast is until 11:30. Lunch is served all day, and coffee and espresso drinks are on from open to close.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Both cafes take orders online, and you can pick yours up when it's ready. Order from Old Port or Brighton Ave.

Do you have gluten-free bread?

Yes, gluten-free bread is one of the choices for breakfast sandwiches and lunch.

Can you handle a big order?

Yes. For orders of six or more, please call the store ahead of time: Old Port (207) 772-8708, Brighton Ave (207) 835-6033.
