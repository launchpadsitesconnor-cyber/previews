About the pub

A pub that keeps the light on.

Come in for a cold beer, a cocktail and a plate of pub food in the heart of Portland. Stop in after dinner, or make us the plan for the whole evening.

What the pub is about

01 · The beer

A mix, with Guinness at one end.

We pour a diverse selection of beers, from Guinness Draught to local favorites like Cold Snap, The Substance and Frye's Leap IPA. The list changes, so call or stop in for what's pouring today. There's a Bloody Mary on the menu too. See the drinks.

02 · The food

Pub staples, built to go with a drink.

The menu leans on the Pub Burger, the Fish Taco, Mac Cheese and baskets of fries. There are also salads, sandwiches, hot dogs and BBQ pulled pork for when you want something quick with your beer. Read the whole menu.

03 · The hours

Open in the morning, lit at midnight.

Doors open at 10:30 AM. Most nights we pour until 1 AM, with one early night: Tuesday, when we close at 11 PM.

Beyond the taps

Food and cocktails, same stool.
