About the pub

Beer, a short menu, a long day.

Commercial St Pub is a bar and pub in Portland. We pour a mix of beers, cook pub food, and open the door at 10:30 in the morning.

The pub

Come in for a cold beer.

A beer, a cocktail, a plate of pub food. Stop in after dinner, or make us the plan for the whole evening.

We pour a diverse selection of beers, from Guinness Draught to local favorites like Cold Snap, The Substance and Frye's Leap IPA.

The food menu keeps things easy with pub staples: the Pub Burger, Fish Taco, Mac Cheese and baskets of fries. There are salads, sandwiches, hot dogs and BBQ pulled pork too, for when you want something quick with your drink.

We're open late most nights, and we open at 10:30 AM, so lunch counts as much as last call.
